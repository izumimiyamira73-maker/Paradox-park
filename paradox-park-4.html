GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked

What's the height requirement for the Drop Protocol?

48 inches (122cm). It's the tallest minimum in the park alongside Flatline.

Is Blackout Sector actually scary, or just dark-themed?

It's built to unsettle, not gore you out — fog, sound design, and timing rather than jump-heavy effects. Nightmare Feed and Static Maze both carry a 13+ guidance, not a hard restriction.

Can I bring outside food?

Sealed snacks and water bottles are fine. No outside meals or coolers — the diners in each zone cover that.

Do I need a Fast Lane add-on?

Only if you're set on Singularity, Web Line, Flatline, or Drop Protocol during peak hours (weekend afternoons). Weekday mornings rarely need it.

What's the refund policy?

Tickets are refundable up to 24 hours before your visit date. Season Passes are non-refundable after activation.

Is there parking?

Yes — general parking is included with every ticket. Preferred parking near the Quantum Quarter entrance is available for an extra fee at the gate.
